Vulnerability-as-a-Service.

Cyber risk rarely starts as one big problem — it starts as one small gap. We run a structured checkup across four layers of your network so you know exactly where that gap is before someone else finds it.

01 / EXTERNAL

External exposure

Are there ports left open by accident? We map what the outside world can actually see and reach.

02 / PERIMETER

Router & firewall

Are dangerous protocols still enabled? We review your edge configuration for the settings attackers look for first.

03 / INTERNAL

Vulnerabilities & encryption

Outdated software or weak encryption is how one small hole becomes a full breach. We find it before it's used against you.

04 / VISIBILITY

Public footprint

Is your network visible on tools like Shodan? We check what's publicly indexed — and how to get it off the map.
